The problem

Design got cheap. Proof didn't.

Generating ten thousand candidate sequences is now a weekend of GPU time. Finding out which ones are real still takes the same weeks it did a decade ago. The bottleneck in AI × Bio has moved from algorithms to validation throughput — and the gap widens every quarter.

The field ends up keeping two ledgers: capability, what a model can demonstrably do in silico, and validity, what survives contact with an actual bench. The first is cheap and easy to game. The second costs money and months, and it is the only one anybody pays for.

We exist to make the second ledger cheap enough and fast enough that a scientist can afford to check.
