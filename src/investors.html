Scale a working autonomous lab

Programmable Bio operates a compact autonomous R&D lab in Hyderabad. The financing case is to expand a functioning execution loop into customer capacity, a standardized assay catalog, and eventually a distributed programmable-biology network.

Investment highlights

  • Operational starting point — a compact autonomous R&D cell is running in Hyderabad today
  • Clear use of capital — instruments, parallel capacity, assay standardization, software, and customer delivery
  • Public price benchmark — launch targets match Ginkgo's published cloud-lab catalog
  • Two customer paths — accessible academic pilots and higher-value industry capacity programs
  • Programmable-biology data loop — structured execution records return evidence to computational design systems
  • Clinical option, honestly scoped — an Apollo collaboration is proposed but no MoU is signed
