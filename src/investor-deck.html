The shift

Design is scaling faster than experimental proof

  • Models generate candidates continuously — proteins, sequences, constructs, and experiment plans are now computational outputs
  • Physical evidence remains scarce — instrument access, method transfer, scheduling, and manual data handling constrain iteration
  • Cloud labs validate the model — Ginkgo and Emerald show that researchers will access laboratory infrastructure remotely
  • The next layer is programmable execution — intent becomes lab operations; results return as structured evidence for the next design cycle

Platform

  • Protocol translation — approved experimental intent becomes versioned instrument operations
  • Capability and quote — requests map to validated methods, materials, capacity, and price
  • Scheduling — plates, instruments, and incubation windows become an optimized execution plan

  • Experimental graph — designs, operations, measurements, and outcomes remain linked for reuse

Clinical option

Apollo is proposed, not contracted

  • No signed MoU today — no EHR, biospecimen, patient, exclusivity, or branding rights are claimed
  • Strategic rationale — a future collaboration could connect reproducible autonomous assays to well-governed, population-relevant research cohorts
  • Required gates — agreement, ethics review, consent, data governance, security, IP, and approved public language
  • Business is independent — Hyderabad lab scaling and customer validation do not depend on Apollo closing
